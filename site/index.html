01 · about

Hardware engineer who takes a block from spec to silicon-ready.

I am a Project Engineer in the R&D group at CDAC Chennai, where I work on the design and verification of a biometric-enabled, indigenous FIDO2 authenticator. My day-to-day spans the full front-end flow: writing synthesizable RTL, building UVM environments with assertions and coverage, closing timing on FPGA, and debugging hardware and firmware together on a RISC-V SoC.

I care about clean micro-architecture, measurable verification closure and security that holds up in hardware. I am the first author of two research papers on threshold and post-quantum FIDO2 authentication.
